Program blueprint

A typical PulseForge week.

Tailor it with your coach, but here is the master template most Lagos and Abuja members follow.

  1. Monday · Strength Priority

    Barbell complexes, sled pushes, and 10-minute finisher. Body scan sync + macros review.

  2. Tuesday · Conditioning & Mobility

    Ride theatre intervals + recovery lab stretch. Breathwork uploaded to app.

  3. Wednesday · Accountability Pod

    Small-group lift recorded for form. Pod dinner reservation auto-booked.

  4. Thursday · Flow or Boxing Night

    Choice of Afrobeats flow, boxing circuits, or virtual check-in if traveling.

  5. Friday · Recovery & Reflection

    Ice barrel, sauna, guided journaling, and weekend plan with coach.
